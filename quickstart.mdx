## Before you begin

You'll need a Web3 wallet (MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet, or any WalletConnect-compatible wallet) and some testnet funds.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Sepolia ETH" icon="ethereum">
    Required for gas fees and the **0.01 ETH operator bond**. Get from a Sepolia
    faucet.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sepolia USDC" icon="coin">
    Required to deposit liquidity as a lender. Mint test USDC from the
    `MockUSDC` contract: `0x17cCeBc2960F50042Fb8f64c18478f083FF0ACDc`
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## I want to lend

Lenders supply USDC to the pool and earn yield as AI agents repay loans.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the TRECC app and connect your wallet">
    Switch your wallet to **Base Sepolia**, then click **Connect Wallet**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Approve USDC spending">
    Approve the `TRECVault` contract to transfer your USDC before depositing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deposit liquidity">
    Enter the amount of USDC you want to supply and confirm the
    `depositLiquidity` transaction. Your share of the pool is recorded
    immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Earn yield">
    Interest accrues automatically as agents repay loans no further action
    needed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  See [Lender Flow](/protocol/lender-flow) for a full breakdown of how yield is
  distributed and what risks to consider.
</Note>

## I want to be an operator

Operators register AI agents on-chain, stake a collateral bond, and pass KYC so their agents can receive loans.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your wallet on Sepolia">
    Open the TRECC app, switch to the **Sepolia** network, and connect your
    wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register an ENS subname">
    Claim a name like `alice.trecc.eth` via the ENS NameWrapper. This is your
    agent's on-chain identity. See [ENS Identity](/concepts/ens-identity).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stake your ETH bond">
    Switch to **Base Sepolia** and stake **0.01 ETH** as collateral. The bond
    protects lenders if your agent defaults.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register your agent">
    Submit `registerAgent(ensName, metadataURI)` on `TRECIdentityRegistry`. A
    soulbound NFT is minted and becomes your agent's permanent ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete KYC">
    TRECC verifies your identity off-chain and issues a signed payload. Submit
    it on-chain to mark your agent as verified.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a loan">
    Once verified, use the TRECC app to submit a loan request. The protocol
    verifies your agent's identity, KYC status, and bond before issuing the
    loan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  See [Operator Flow](/protocol/operator-flow) for details on bond slashing,
  credit scores, and the full set of on-chain checks before a loan is issued.
</Note>
