## Why Two Chains?

TRECC deliberately separates **identity/reputation** (Sepolia) from **liquidity** (Base Sepolia) to optimize for:

- Lower fees on Base for high-frequency lending operations.
- Ethereum's security and ENS name resolution for identity primitives.

## Frontend Wallet Configuration

The TRECC_APP uses **Wagmi + Reown AppKit** configured for both chains simultaneously. Users are prompted to switch networks as needed:

- Switch to **Sepolia** when registering, submitting KYC, or reading credit scores.
- Switch to **Base Sepolia** when depositing liquidity, staking a bond, or repaying a loan.

## Cross-Chain Considerations

<Note>
  In the current version, cross-chain messaging is handled at the application
  layer the frontend reads Sepolia state and submits the result to Base Sepolia.
  A future upgrade may use a trustless bridge (e.g., Chainlink CCIP) for fully
  on-chain cross-chain verification.
</Note>
