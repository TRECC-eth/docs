## The Hallucination Problem

As the machine economy scales, AI agents are increasingly executing high-stakes financial transactions on-chain. However, Large Language Models (LLMs) are inherently probabilistic. They guess, they hallucinate, and they execute on stale context. 

Giving a probabilistic model direct access to raw private keys or unconstrained capital introduces catastrophic risk. Traditional human-in-the-loop approvals (like a manual multisig click) solve this, but they are too slow for agents operating on 2-second block times.

**TRECC’s core philosophy: We do not try to fix the AI model. We secure the execution layer.**

Instead of trusting an AI not to hallucinate, TRECC intercepts the AI's financial intent and enforces **100% deterministic mathematical guardrails** before any capital ever moves.

## The Guardrail Pipeline

When an AI agent (orchestrated via frameworks like Letta or OpenServ) decides to execute a trade, TRECC's infrastructure steps in. The process operates in milliseconds:

### 1. Intent Interception
The AI formulates a trading strategy and generates a transaction payload. Before this payload reaches the signing enclave, TRECC's backend intercepts the probabilistic intent.

### 2. Constraint Validation
The requested transaction is run through our proprietary Risk Engine. The engine evaluates the payload against strict mathematical parameters, including:
* **Protocol Whitelists:** Is the target protocol (e.g., Aave, Compound) approved?
* **Capital Limits:** Does the transaction size exceed the agent's staked safety bond or dynamic credit limit?
* **Slippage & State Revalidation:** Does the current on-chain liquidity state match the AI's assumptions, or is it operating on stale authority?

### 3. The Kill Switch
If the AI hallucinates, attempts to interact with an unverified contract, or violates any risk parameter, the Guardrail Layer triggers an instant **Kill Switch**. The transaction is dropped off-chain, costing zero gas and preventing any capital loss.

### 4. Machine-Speed Execution
If the math checks out, the payload is securely passed to **Turnkey’s Trusted Execution Environments (TEEs)**, which programmatically sign the transaction in sub-100ms and route it to the agent's **Safe (Gnosis) Vault** for on-chain settlement.

## Verifiable Compute & Starknet STARKs

To ensure institutional-grade security without compromising on speed or privacy, TRECC's guardrails are deeply integrated with **Starknet's cryptographic infrastructure**.

We cannot process complex, multi-variable risk checks on the Ethereum mainnet—it is too slow and exposes proprietary trading logic to front-runners. Instead, TRECC utilizes **STARK (Scalable Transparent Arguments of Knowledge)** proofs.

* **Off-Chain Processing:** TRECC calculates the risk parameters and validates the AI's intent off-chain.
* **Cryptographic Proofs:** We generate a STARK proof that mathematically guarantees the transaction obeys all predefined guardrails and credit limits.
* **On-Chain Verification:** The smart contract only needs to verify the tiny STARK proof. This turns the probabilistic guess of an AI into a deterministic, cryptographically proven action.

By leveraging STARKs, we ensure that no transaction can be executed unless it mathematically proves its own safety.

## Agentic Shielding & Privacy (Roadmap)

A critical component of TRECC's long-term guardrail system is the protection of trading logic. If an AI agent's constraints and parameters are fully public, MEV bots and competitors can easily reverse-engineer the agent's strategy or front-run its trades.

To solve this, TRECC is actively building towards an integration with **Starknet's STRK20 framework** and Zero-Knowledge (ZK) execution environments. In our upcoming architectural iterations, this integration will enable:

1. **Shielded Constraints:** The exact risk weights and proprietary constraints will remain entirely shielded from public view.
2. **Privacy Pools:** The agent's treasury balances and transfer histories will be obscured via Starknet privacy pools.
3. **Anonymous Compliance:** The underlying user backing the AI agent is verified (via Reclaim Protocol ZK proofs) but never doxed on a public ledger.
